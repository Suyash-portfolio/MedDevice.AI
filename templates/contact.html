Frequently Asked Questions

What is MedDevice.AI? ▾

MedDevice.AI is an AI-powered medical device assistant that helps healthcare professionals with device compatibility checks, regulatory compliance, and clinical decision support using real-time data analysis.

How is my data protected and kept private? ▾

We follow HIPAA and GDPR guidelines. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit using AES-256 and TLS 1.3. We never share personally identifiable information without explicit consent and undergo annual third-party security audits.

How accurate are the AI predictions? ▾

Our models achieve over 97% accuracy on benchmark clinical datasets. We continuously train on de-identified data from certified sources and provide confidence scores so clinicians can make informed decisions.

Which medical devices are currently supported? ▾

We support over 2,000 devices from major manufacturers including GE Healthcare, Siemens Healthineers, Philips, Medtronic, and Boston Scientific. Coverage spans imaging, monitoring, diagnostics, and surgical robotics.

What does the pricing look like? ▾

We offer a free tier for individual practitioners and scalable enterprise plans for hospitals and clinics. Contact our sales team for a personalized quote with no commitment required.
